| --- | --- |

| [Google Maps Scraper](../apis/google-maps-scraper.md) | Local business discovery |
| [LeadLocator Pro](../apis/leadlocator-pro.md) | Local lead list creation |
| [Y Combinator Scraper](../apis/y-combinator-scraper.md) | Startup account discovery |
| [Crunchbase Companies Scraper](../apis/crunchbase-companies-scraper.md) | Funded company lists |
| [Amazon Seller Data Extractor](../apis/amazon-seller-data-extractor.md) | Marketplace seller discovery |
| [Zillow Contact API](../apis/zillow-contact-api.md) | Real estate lead discovery |
