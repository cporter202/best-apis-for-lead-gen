# Real Estate Investor Lead Finder

- One-line summary: A market-specific FSBO and property owner lead finder for real estate investors.
- Customer: Investors, agents, wholesalers, real estate lead gen services.
- Pain point: Real estate buyers need timely property leads and contact workflows.
- Product or service idea: Pull property leads, score by investor criteria, and route to a compliant follow-up queue.
- APIs used: Zillow Contact API, AI Lead Qualifier, Easy Bulk Email Validator.
- MVP feature list: Market search, property filters, contact fields, qualification tags, saved lists, follow-up status.
- Monetization model: Subscription by market, pay-per-lead, or local exclusivity packages.
- Pricing ideas: $99-$499/month per market depending on lead quality and freshness.
- Build difficulty: Advanced.
- Time to launch: 3-5 weeks.
- Go-to-market idea: Start with one city and sell to local investors before scaling.
- Why it works: Real estate leads have obvious transaction value.
- Risks / caveats: Real estate outreach is compliance-sensitive; platform terms need careful review.
