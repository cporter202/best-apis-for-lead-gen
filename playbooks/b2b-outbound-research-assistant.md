# B2B Outbound Research Assistant

- One-line summary: An enrichment and research assistant that turns company domains into qualified account briefs.
- Customer: B2B sales teams, SDR managers, founders, agencies.
- Pain point: Reps waste time researching accounts and filling missing CRM fields.
- Product or service idea: Input a company list and receive enriched contacts, company summary, ICP fit, and pitch notes.
- APIs used: AI Contact Details Scraper, Clearbit Combined, Hunter.io, AI Lead Qualifier, Smart Email Finder and Verifier.
- MVP feature list: CSV upload, enrichment waterfall, ICP score, missing email finder, account brief export.
- Monetization model: Usage-based SaaS or agency research service.
- Pricing ideas: $0.05-$0.50 per enriched record, or $199-$999/month.
- Build difficulty: Intermediate.
- Time to launch: 2-4 weeks.
- Go-to-market idea: Target founder-led sales teams that already have domain lists but weak enrichment.
- Why it works: It improves existing outbound workflows without requiring a new sales process.
- Risks / caveats: Third-party enrichment coverage varies; keep source confidence visible.
