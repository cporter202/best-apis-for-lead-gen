# Contractor Lead Intelligence Dashboard

- One-line summary: A dashboard that ranks local contractors by website quality, reviews, and contactability.
- Customer: Agencies selling websites, SEO, reviews, ads, or CRM setup to contractors.
- Pain point: Contractor agencies need to know which businesses are worth contacting first.
- Product or service idea: Pull contractors from maps/directories, enrich contact data, score them by review and website signals.
- APIs used: Google Maps Scraper, Google Maps Email Extractor, Trustpilot Reviews Scraper, BuiltWith Bulk URLs, AI Lead Qualifier.
- MVP feature list: Category search, city filters, review/rating fields, website stack tags, lead score, export.
- Monetization model: Vertical SaaS or monthly prospecting reports.
- Pricing ideas: $99/month for one market, $299/month for multi-market agencies.
- Build difficulty: Intermediate.
- Time to launch: 2-3 weeks.
- Go-to-market idea: Start with one niche like HVAC, roofing, electricians, or plumbers.
- Why it works: Contractors are high-LTV customers for agencies, and public signals make prioritization easier.
- Risks / caveats: Review data coverage varies; do not overstate inferred business pain.
