# Review Monitoring Lead Engine

- One-line summary: Find companies with review pain that maps to a specific service offer.
- Customer: Reputation agencies, CX consultants, support software companies, competitor displacement teams.
- Pain point: Review pain is public but hard to monitor across many accounts.
- Product or service idea: Scrape and classify reviews, detect recurring pain, enrich the company, and produce an account brief.
- APIs used: Trustpilot Reviews Scraper, AI Lead Qualifier, AI Contact Details Scraper, Easy Bulk Email Validator.
- MVP feature list: Company/watchlist import, review scrape, pain classifier, severity score, enriched contact fields, alert feed.
- Monetization model: Monitoring subscription or done-for-you prospect reports.
- Pricing ideas: $99-$499/month for monitoring; $500+ for custom competitor reports.
- Build difficulty: Intermediate.
- Time to launch: 2-4 weeks.
- Go-to-market idea: Sell a "competitor complaint report" to agencies and SaaS sales teams.
- Why it works: Reviews provide specific language for positioning and qualification.
- Risks / caveats: Keep outreach respectful and avoid referencing sensitive individual reviewers.
