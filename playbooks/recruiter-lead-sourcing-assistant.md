# Recruiter Lead Sourcing Assistant

- One-line summary: Monitor job postings to find companies that may need recruiting or staffing help.
- Customer: Recruiters, staffing firms, fractional talent teams.
- Pain point: Recruiters need timing signals, not generic company lists.
- Product or service idea: Track hiring spikes by role, location, or industry and enrich company contacts.
- APIs used: Advanced LinkedIn Job Search API, LinkedIn Jobs Scraper with Company Insights, All Jobs Scraper, AI Contact Details Scraper.
- MVP feature list: Job query builder, company dedupe, hiring volume score, contact enrichment, saved alerts.
- Monetization model: Monthly SaaS, done-for-you alerts, or recruiting agency internal tool.
- Pricing ideas: $99-$499/month depending on alert volume and seats.
- Build difficulty: Intermediate.
- Time to launch: 2-3 weeks.
- Go-to-market idea: Start with one niche such as healthcare, software sales, accounting, or skilled trades.
- Why it works: Active hiring creates immediate context for a recruiter or staffing pitch.
- Risks / caveats: Job data needs dedupe and freshness handling.
