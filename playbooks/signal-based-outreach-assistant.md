# Signal-Based Outreach Assistant

- One-line summary: Monitor social, job, funding, and review signals to prioritize accounts.
- Customer: B2B SaaS teams, agencies, social sellers, consultants.
- Pain point: Generic cold lists lack timing and relevance.
- Product or service idea: Pull public signals, classify them by intent, and create account research notes.
- APIs used: Reddit Searcher, Tweet Scraper V2, Company Funding Details Scraper, Advanced LinkedIn Job Search API, Trustpilot Reviews Scraper.
- MVP feature list: Signal sources, keyword rules, account matching, urgency scoring, Slack/email alerts, CRM export.
- Monetization model: Monthly monitoring subscription.
- Pricing ideas: $99-$599/month depending on sources and alert volume.
- Build difficulty: Intermediate to advanced.
- Time to launch: 3-5 weeks.
- Go-to-market idea: Launch with one wedge signal, such as hiring spikes or competitor complaints.
- Why it works: Timely outreach performs better when tied to a visible business event.
- Risks / caveats: Matching social signals to real accounts can be noisy.
