# Local Lead Scraper For Agencies

- One-line summary: A repeatable tool that finds, enriches, and exports local SMB leads by niche and city.
- Customer: Local SEO, web design, ads, and appointment-setting agencies.
- Pain point: Agencies need fresh local prospect lists without hours of manual map and website research.
- Product or service idea: A simple app where users enter niche plus location and receive enriched local business leads.
- APIs used: Google Maps Scraper, Google Maps Email Extractor, AI Contact Details Scraper, Easy Bulk Email Validator.
- MVP feature list: Search form, duplicate removal, contact extraction, validation status, CSV export, saved campaigns.
- Monetization model: Subscription plus usage credits, or done-for-you lead packs.
- Pricing ideas: $49-$199/month for small agencies; $250-$1,000/month for white-label or high-volume lists.
- Build difficulty: Beginner to intermediate.
- Time to launch: 1-2 weeks for a useful MVP.
- Go-to-market idea: Publish niche demos such as "roofers in Phoenix with emails" and sell to agencies.
- Why it works: The buyer already understands the value of fresh local prospect lists.
- Risks / caveats: Contact data can be incomplete; outreach must comply with local law and email rules.
