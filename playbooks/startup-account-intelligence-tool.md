# Startup Account Intelligence Tool

- One-line summary: Build account maps for startups by funding, batch, hiring, team size, and founder data.
- Customer: Startup-focused SaaS teams, agencies, recruiters, investors.
- Pain point: Startup prospecting requires stage context and founder/company research.
- Product or service idea: Aggregate YC and Crunchbase-style startup records into account briefs and lead lists.
- APIs used: Y Combinator Scraper, Crunchbase Companies Scraper, Company Funding Details Scraper, Crunchbase Email Scraper.
- MVP feature list: Startup search, filters, account profile, founder/contact enrichment, funding/hiring tags, export.
- Monetization model: SaaS subscription or custom research reports.
- Pricing ideas: $99-$399/month for SaaS teams; custom lists at $500+.
- Build difficulty: Intermediate.
- Time to launch: 2-4 weeks.
- Go-to-market idea: Build around a specific audience such as YC companies hiring engineers.
- Why it works: Startups are easy to segment by stage, market, and timing.
- Risks / caveats: Some startup contact data may require manual review.
