# Directory Mining Workflow

- Goal: Turn niche directories into clean, enriched lead lists.
- Who it is for: Agencies, partnership teams, B2B researchers, niche data sellers.
- APIs used: [Advanced Yellowpages Scraper](../apis/advanced-yellowpages-scraper.md), [192.com Businesses Scraper](../apis/192-com-businesses-scraper.md), [Advanced Clutch.co Scraper](../apis/advanced-clutch-co-scraper.md), [AI Contact Details Scraper](../apis/ai-contact-details-scraper.md), [Easy Bulk Email Validator](../apis/easy-bulk-email-validator.md).
- Inputs: Directory URL/search, category, geography, minimum profile completeness.
- Outputs: Company records, category, website, phone, social links, emails, validation status.

## Steps

1. Choose a directory that maps tightly to a buyer segment.
2. Scrape profiles by category and location.
3. Normalize names, domains, phones, and locations.
4. Enrich missing website and contact fields.
5. Validate emails and remove duplicates.
6. Package the list by niche and offer.

## Why It Is Valuable

Directories are often pre-segmented by niche, which makes them strong lead sources when cleaned properly.

## Monetization Angle

Sell niche lead packs, agency prospect lists, or directory-based CRM imports.

## Complexity

Beginner to intermediate.
