# Intent Research To Outreach Workflow

- Goal: Turn public discussions into market research and prospecting triggers.
- Who it is for: SaaS founders, agencies, consultants, social selling teams.
- APIs used: [Reddit Searcher](../apis/reddit-searcher.md), [Tweet Scraper V2](../apis/tweet-scraper-v2.md), [AI Lead Qualifier](../apis/ai-lead-qualifier.md), [Hunter.io](../apis/hunter-io.md).
- Inputs: Keywords, competitor names, pain phrases, subreddits, X/Twitter lists, profile filters.
- Outputs: Posts, themes, accounts/profiles, possible companies, research notes, outreach angles.

## Steps

1. Search public discussions for pain points or competitor mentions.
2. Cluster posts by problem, urgency, and audience.
3. Identify accounts, creators, or companies worth researching.
4. Enrich company domains and contacts where appropriate.
5. Create non-spammy outreach or content ideas based on the research.

## Why It Is Valuable

It finds demand before prospects fill out a form.

## Monetization Angle

Build social intent alerts, market research reports, or account research workflows.

## Complexity

Intermediate.
