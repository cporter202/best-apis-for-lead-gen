# Review-Triggered Sales Workflow

- Goal: Use review data to find companies with visible customer pain or reputation opportunities.
- Who it is for: Reputation agencies, support software sellers, CX consultants, competitor displacement teams.
- APIs used: [Trustpilot Reviews Scraper](../apis/trustpilot-reviews-scraper.md), [AI Lead Qualifier](../apis/ai-lead-qualifier.md), [AI Contact Details Scraper](../apis/ai-contact-details-scraper.md), [Easy Bulk Email Validator](../apis/easy-bulk-email-validator.md).
- Inputs: Company domains, competitor names, review thresholds, keywords, rating filters.
- Outputs: Companies, review excerpts, pain themes, severity score, contact data, suggested pitch angle.

## Steps

1. Scrape reviews for target companies or competitors.
2. Identify negative themes, recurring complaints, and recent spikes.
3. Enrich target company websites with contact data.
4. Qualify whether the account fits the offer.
5. Prioritize accounts with urgent or recurring review pain.
6. Create research-backed outreach notes.

## Why It Is Valuable

Reviews reveal public pain in the customer's own language.

## Monetization Angle

Offer reputation audits, CX consulting lead lists, or competitor complaint monitoring.

## Complexity

Beginner to intermediate.
