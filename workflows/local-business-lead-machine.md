# Local Business Lead Machine

- Goal: Build clean local business lead lists by niche and location.
- Who it is for: Agencies, local SEO sellers, web design shops, appointment setters.
- APIs used: [Google Maps Scraper](../apis/google-maps-scraper.md), [Google Maps Email Extractor](../apis/google-maps-email-extractor.md), [AI Contact Details Scraper](../apis/ai-contact-details-scraper.md), [Easy Bulk Email Validator](../apis/easy-bulk-email-validator.md), [AI Lead Qualifier](../apis/ai-lead-qualifier.md).
- Inputs: Keyword, city/region, service category, minimum rating, optional website-required filter.
- Outputs: Business name, address, phone, website, email, social links, category, rating, qualification notes, CRM-ready CSV.

## Steps

1. Search for businesses by niche and geography.
2. Deduplicate by business name, phone, and domain.
3. Extract website contact details for each business.
4. Validate emails and remove risky records.
5. Qualify the business from its website and listing data.
6. Export segmented lists by niche, city, and offer fit.

## Why It Is Valuable

Most agencies need a reliable way to create targeted local prospect lists without manually searching maps and websites.

## Monetization Angle

Sell as monthly local lead packs, a white-label lead scraper, or a done-for-you prospecting service.

## Complexity

Beginner to intermediate.
