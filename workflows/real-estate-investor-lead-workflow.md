# Real Estate Investor Lead Workflow

- Goal: Find real estate leads and qualify them for investor, wholesaler, or agent follow-up.
- Who it is for: Real estate investors, agents, wholesalers, property lead gen tools.
- APIs used: [Zillow Contact API](../apis/zillow-contact-api.md), [AI Lead Qualifier](../apis/ai-lead-qualifier.md), [Easy Bulk Email Validator](../apis/easy-bulk-email-validator.md).
- Inputs: Geography, property type, listing status, FSBO indicators, price filters.
- Outputs: Property record, owner/agent contact fields where available, qualification notes, follow-up status.

## Steps

1. Pull FSBO or owner/agent lead data for a target market.
2. Filter by property type, price band, and listing status.
3. Add qualification notes and investor criteria.
4. Deduplicate against existing property/contact records.
5. Route promising leads into a compliant follow-up workflow.

## Why It Is Valuable

Real estate prospecting has a clear revenue model when the lead source is timely and market-specific.

## Monetization Angle

Sell investor lead feeds, FSBO alerts, or niche market lead dashboards.

## Complexity

Advanced.
