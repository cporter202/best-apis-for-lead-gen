# Ecommerce Seller Prospecting Engine

- Goal: Find ecommerce sellers and enrich them for agency or SaaS outreach.
- Who it is for: Ecommerce agencies, Amazon consultants, Shopify app founders, marketplace SaaS teams.
- APIs used: [Amazon Seller Data Extractor](../apis/amazon-seller-data-extractor.md), [EU Amazon Sellers Email Leads](../apis/eu-amazon-sellers-email-leads.md), [EU eBay Sellers Email Leads](../apis/eu-ebay-sellers-email-leads.md), [AI Contact Details Scraper](../apis/ai-contact-details-scraper.md), [Easy Bulk Email Validator](../apis/easy-bulk-email-validator.md).
- Inputs: Marketplace, product category, region, seller size indicators, optional target service.
- Outputs: Seller name, storefront URL, marketplace, product signals, contact fields, validation status, offer angle.

## Steps

1. Pull seller records from marketplace sources or seed databases.
2. Filter by marketplace, category, seller rating, product volume, or region.
3. Enrich seller websites or domains for emails, phones, and social links.
4. Validate emails and remove duplicates across marketplaces.
5. Add segmentation tags such as "Amazon-only," "multi-marketplace," or "high review count."
6. Export to CRM or a review queue.

## Why It Is Valuable

Ecommerce sellers buy services for ads, listings, logistics, reviews, storefront design, and marketplace expansion.

## Monetization Angle

Sell seller intelligence, build an ecommerce agency prospecting dashboard, or package niche seller lists.

## Complexity

Intermediate.
