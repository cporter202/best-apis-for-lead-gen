# Technology Stack Prospecting Workflow

- Goal: Find companies using or missing specific technologies.
- Who it is for: SaaS founders, migration agencies, web development shops, integration consultants.
- APIs used: [BuiltWith Bulk URLs](../apis/builtwith-bulk-urls.md), [WhatRuns](../apis/whatruns.md), [Google Maps Scraper](../apis/google-maps-scraper.md), [AI Contact Details Scraper](../apis/ai-contact-details-scraper.md), [Smart Email Finder and Verifier](../apis/smart-email-finder-verifier.md).
- Inputs: Domain list, target technology, exclusion technology, company niche, location.
- Outputs: Domains, detected stack, technology tags, contacts, fit score, recommended offer.

## Steps

1. Build a domain list from maps, directories, ecommerce sellers, or CRM exports.
2. Detect technologies in bulk.
3. Segment by current stack, missing stack, outdated stack, or competitor stack.
4. Enrich domains with contacts and emails.
5. Validate email fields and dedupe by company.
6. Create offer-specific outreach lists.

## Why It Is Valuable

Technology usage creates concrete reasons to reach out: migration, integration, optimization, compliance, or replacement.

## Monetization Angle

Build a SaaS prospecting tool, sell migration campaigns, or offer technology audit lead lists.

## Complexity

Intermediate.
