# Startup Founder Prospecting Workflow

- Goal: Build targeted startup and founder lists from startup data sources.
- Who it is for: B2B SaaS teams, recruiters, startup agencies, investors.
- APIs used: [Y Combinator Scraper](../apis/y-combinator-scraper.md), [Crunchbase Companies Scraper](../apis/crunchbase-companies-scraper.md), [Company Funding Details Scraper](../apis/company-funding-details-scraper.md), [Crunchbase Email Scraper](../apis/crunchbase-email-scraper.md), [Hunter.io](../apis/hunter-io.md).
- Inputs: Industry, batch, geography, funding stage, hiring status, company size.
- Outputs: Startup accounts, founders, funding context, hiring indicators, emails where available, account notes.

## Steps

1. Pull startup accounts from YC and/or Crunchbase sources.
2. Filter by industry, stage, batch, geography, or hiring status.
3. Add funding context and recent account signals.
4. Find founder or company emails using enrichment APIs.
5. Validate and segment by offer fit.
6. Export founder-focused account lists.

## Why It Is Valuable

Startups often have clear stage-based needs and visible growth signals.

## Monetization Angle

Sell startup account intelligence, founder outreach lists, or a founder research assistant.

## Complexity

Intermediate.
