# Outreach Compliance Notes

This repo is for building useful lead generation systems, not spam tools.

## Practical Guardrails

- Follow each API provider's terms and the rules of the underlying platform.
- Respect applicable privacy, email, phone, and consumer protection laws.
- Validate emails and remove risky or invalid contacts before sending.
- Keep opt-out handling and suppression lists in any outreach workflow.
- Use public signals for relevance and prioritization, not invasive personalization.
- Avoid scraping or contacting people in sensitive contexts.
- Do not assume that publicly visible data is automatically fair game for any use.

## Product Design Advice

Make compliance visible in the workflow. Good lead generation products should include validation status, source URLs, confidence notes, suppression lists, and clear export boundaries.
