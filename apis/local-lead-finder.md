# Local Lead Finder

- Link: [Local Lead Finder](https://apimesa.com)

- Best lead gen use cases: Local business prospecting, agency client sourcing, franchise lead lists.
- Ideal customer or builder: Marketing agencies, local sales teams, directory builders.
- Data provided: Names, addresses, phones, and websites pulled live from OpenStreetMap data.

- Weaknesses: Results depend on source data quality; validate a sample on your own use case before scaling spend.
- Best workflow fit: Geographic lead mining feeding local outreach.
- Difficulty level: Beginner.
