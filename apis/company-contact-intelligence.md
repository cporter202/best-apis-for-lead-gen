# Company & Contact Intelligence

- Best lead gen use cases: Company enrichment, account research, ICP-fit scoring.
- Ideal customer or builder: Sales teams, B2B agencies, data product builders.
- Data provided: Public business identity, emails, phones, addresses, social profiles, technologies, and contact-page evidence.

- Best workflow fit: Enrichment layer after company list building.
